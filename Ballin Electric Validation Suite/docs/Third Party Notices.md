# Third party notices and license tracking

No third-party executables, libraries, icons or proprietary diagnostic tools are bundled in this planning package. Reference product names identify inspiration only and do not imply affiliation.

Before adding any dependency, record name, version, source, license, commercial use rights, redistribution terms, notices, relevant SDK/driver terms and review status. Verify FLTK and FLTK-rs obligations, including applicable static-linking terms. Maintain a dependency manifest and SBOM when the Cargo workspace exists.

Do not bundle Prime95, FurMark, OCCT, PC-Doctor, HWiNFO, PassMark or other proprietary tools without confirming rights for the specific intended use. Open-source dependencies also carry obligations. Do not select a public project license without the owner's approval.
